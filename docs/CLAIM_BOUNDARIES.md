# Claim Boundaries

## Supported exact claims

- `Conv(P x {-1,0,+1}) = P x [-1,1]` for convex nonempty P.
- The recursive family from a point is the n-cube `[-1,1]^n`.
- `f_k = 2^(n-k) * binom(n,k)`.
- The number of nonempty cubical faces including the polytope is `3^n`.
- The legacy octagonal vertex interpretation is incompatible with the stated operator.
- The exact adversarial examples and discriminant-wall determinants in the supplement.

## Supported finite-experiment claims

- Quotient canonicalizers passed all stated positive controls in the reference run.
- Reported collision/reconstructibility values hold for the generated atlas and operational target.
- Arithmetic multiplicity profile was collision-free in the 2000-object atlas.
- Ehrhart signatures are highly discriminative but admit exact adversarial structural collisions.
- Unpolarized toric variety is too coarse for the controlled box family; polarization/Ehrhart data distinguish the enumerated classes.

## Explicitly not claimed

- Universal completeness/minimality of the arithmetic multiplicity profile.
- A new zonotope/polytope family under the original operator.
- Ricci-flatness, special holonomy, Calabi-Yau status, M-theory compactification, or physical unification.
- Biological or neural identity, consciousness, brain-death criteria, or clinical validation.
- Q1 journal acceptance. “Q1-level” here means the package is structured to the rigor and reproducibility expectations of a strong mathematical/computational journal, not that a quartile outcome is guaranteed.
